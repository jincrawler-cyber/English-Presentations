The Simple Past

Memories & Finished Actions

What is different?

Group A ⏱️ Present

  • I am in Aguascalientes.
  • She eats chascas.
  • They play soccer today.

Group B ⏪ Past

  • I was in Aguascalientes.
  • She ate chascas yesterday.
  • They played soccer last week.

Two Groups The King vs. The Citizens

In the past, we have two very different groups of verbs. They don't mix!

👑

Verb "To Be"

It's the King. It doesn't need help for negatives or questions.

was / were

wasn't / weren't

👥

All Other Verbs

They are normal citizens. They need the helper "DID".

played / went

didn't play / didn't go

Other Verbs Regular vs. Irregular

🔄 Regular Verbs (+ed)

Easy! Just add -ed or -d at the end.

  • play ➔ played
  • work ➔ worked
  • dance ➔ danced
  • study ➔ studied

🤪 Irregular Verbs

Crazy! They change completely. You have to memorize them.

  • go ➔ went
  • eat ➔ ate
  • have ➔ had
  • buy ➔ bought

Pronunciation How to say "-ed"

Many students say "play-ed" or "work-ed". Don't do that! Here is the simple secret:

🔊

Rule 1: Extra Syllable (/id/)

Only add an extra syllable if the original verb ends in a T or D sound.

want ➔ want-ed need ➔ need-ed
🔇

Rule 2: NO Extra Syllable (/t/ or /d/)

For all other verbs, the "e" is silent. Just make a "t" or "d" sound at the end.

play ➔ play'd watch ➔ watch't clean ➔ clean'd

Grammar (+) and (-)

✅ Affirmative

Subject + V2 (Past verb)
  • • I went to the cinema.
  • • She played tennis.
  • • We were happy.

❌ Negative

Sub + didn't + V1 (Base)
*Didn't eats the past!
  • • I didn't go to the cinema.
  • • She didn't play tennis.
  • • We weren't happy. (To Be is King!)

Questions (?)

How do we ask questions in the past? Remember our two groups!

👥 Normal Verbs

Did + sub + V1 (Base) ?

"Did you eat tacos?"

✅ Yes, I did. / ❌ No, I didn't.

👑 Verb To Be

Was/Were + sub + ... ?

"Were you tired?"

✅ Yes, I was. / ❌ No, I wasn't.
