Determiners

All, Most, Many, Some, None

Read these sentences.
What is the difference?

🌍 Group A

All sales clerks are friendly.
Some people don't like shopping.
No customers like high prices.

🎯 Group B

All of the sales clerks here are friendly.

The Rule General vs. Specific

We use different structures if we are talking about the whole world (General) or a small, specific group.

🌍

General

Things/People in the world

Determiner + Noun

"Most stores have good sales."

(All the stores in the world)

🎯

Specific

Things/People in this group

Determiner + of the + Noun

"Most of the stores in my town..."

(Only the stores in this specific town)

Pronouns Us, You, Them

If we already know who the group is, we don't repeat the noun. We use object pronouns!

Determiner + OF + us / you / them
👥

All of them were on the middle shelf.

🙋‍♂️🙋‍♀️

Most of us like learning English.

*Notice we say "of them", NOT "of the them". Pronouns never use "the"!

DANGER: No vs. None

This is the biggest mistake students make! They don't mix!

NO

Use it directly with a noun.

✅ "I have no eggs."
❌ "I have none eggs."

NONE

Use it with OF THE or by itself.

✅ "None of the eggs are big."
✅ "How many eggs? None."
