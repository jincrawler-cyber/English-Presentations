All, Most, Many, Some, None

Specific Things vs. General Things

100%

Compare these two situations...

Situation A: The World

"Most people work."

(In general, everywhere)

Situation B: My Office

"Most of the people here work hard."

(Specific group of people)

What is the small word difference? "OF THE"

The Rule: General vs. Specific

GENERAL

Talk about things in the whole world.

Quantifier + Noun

All cars...

Most people...

SPECIFIC

Talk about a specific group (mine, yours, these).

Quantifier + OF + THE/MY + Noun

All of my cars...

Most of the people...

Example: Children

General

"All children like to play."

(Every child in the world)

Specific

"Some of those children are sad."

(Only that specific group)

Zero Quantity: No vs. None

NO + Noun

Use before the noun directly.

"I have no money."

"There is no food."

(Don't say: None money)

NONE OF + Group

Use when talking about a specific group.

"None of my friends came."

"None of the answers were correct."

Quiz: Choose the correct one

"________ players in this team are very tall."

Summary Cheat Sheet

To say... General Structure Specific Structure
100% All cats... All (of) my cats...
80% Most people... Most of the people...
30-50% Some birds... Some of those birds...
0% No students... None of the students...

Tip: If you see "The", "My", "These" -> Use "OF"!
