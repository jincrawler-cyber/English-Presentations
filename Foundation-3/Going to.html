They are talking about plans for the future!

The Rule When do we use it?

We use "Going to" when we talk about our ideas for the future. You decided this BEFORE speaking.

🗓️ Future Plans

In your calendar

"I bought the tickets. I am going to travel to Cancun on Friday."
💭 Intentions

In your mind

"My room is a mess. I am going to clean it this afternoon."

The Formula 🧪

"Going to" never works alone. It always needs its best friend: the verb TO BE!

✅

Affirmative (+)

Subject + am/is/are + going to + base verb
  • • I am going to study tonight.
  • • She is going to watch a movie.
  • • They are going to play soccer.
❌

Negative (-)

Just add NOT to the verb To Be!

  • • I 'm not going to study.
  • • She isn't going to watch a movie.
  • • They aren't going to play.

Questions Asking about plans

To make a question, just switch the Subject and the verb TO BE!

Am / Is / Are + subject + going to + base verb ?
"Are you going to travel tomorrow?"
✅ Yes, I am.
❌ No, I 'm not.
