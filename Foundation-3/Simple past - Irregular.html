The Past Simple

Two Types of Verbs

🤖 Regulars
👾 Irregulars

Click "Next" to investigate.

Group A: The Robots 🤖

Look at these sentences about yesterday.

1. I played football yesterday.
2. She cooked dinner last night.
3. We watched TV on Sunday.

❓ What do these words have in common?

They all end in -ED!

Group B: The Rebels 👾

Now look at these sentences.

1. I went to the park. (Go)
2. He ate a pizza. (Eat)
3. They saw a movie. (See)

❓ Do they end in -ED?

NO! They change completely.

Let's Compare

Regulars 🤖

They follow the rule.

Play + ed
Work + ed
Visit + ed

Irregulars 👾

They are crazy! No rules.

Go ➡️ Went
Buy ➡️ Bought
Have ➡️ Had
