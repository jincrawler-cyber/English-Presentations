Connecting Tenses

Present Perfect vs Simple Past

Read this conversation.
Notice the change!

Have you ever been to Mexico?
Yes, I have!
Oh, nice! When did you go?
I went there last year.
Did you like the food?
Yes! I ate so many tacos. It was delicious!

The Rule General vs Specific

We use the two tenses together to tell a story.

🌍

1. Present Perfect

To Open The Topic

We use it to talk about general life experiences. The exact time doesn't matter.

"Have you ever eaten sushi?"
➔
🔎

2. Simple Past

To Give Details

We use it to ask or talk about specific details (when, where, who, why).

"Where did you eat it? Was it good?"

The Conversation Funnel

Present Perfect (General)

"I have seen that movie."

Wait, I need details...

Simple Past (Specific)

"When did you see it?"

"I saw it yesterday."

Wh- Questions Asking for details

When your partner says "Yes, I have", use these Simple Past questions to keep the conversation going!

Where were you?

Who did you go with?

Did you like it? ❤️

Did you like it?

Was it fun?

🗣️ Interview Time

Work in pairs. Ask your partner these questions. If they say "Yes", act like a detective and ask for at least 2 specific details!

  • • Have you ever traveled by airplane?
  • • Have you ever eaten something very strange?

> When did you...? > Where were you? > What happened? > Did it hurt? > Did you like it?
