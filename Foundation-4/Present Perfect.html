Life Experiences

Using the Present Perfect

Read these sentences.
What is different?

Group A 📅 Specific Time

  • I visited London in 2019.
  • She ate sushi yesterday.
Past Simple

Group B 🌍 Life Experience

  • I have visited London.
  • She has eaten sushi.
Present Perfect (When? It doesn't matter!)

Structure The Formula

We use the auxiliary Have/Has + The Past Participle (V3) of the verb.

The Auxiliary

I / You / We / They have ('ve)
He / She / It has ('s)

The Verb (V3)

  • visit ➔ visited
  • eat ➔ eaten
  • see ➔ seen
  • go ➔ been / gone

Questions Using "Ever"

When we ask about life experiences, we often use the word EVER (at any time in your life).

Have/Has + subject + ever + Past Participle ?
"Have you ever eaten octopus?"
✅ Yes, I have.
❌ No, I haven't.

Negatives Using "Never"

If you want to say "No" to an experience, you can use NEVER. But be careful with the grammar!

✅ The Correct Way

"I have never seen a ghost."

*The auxiliary 'have' is positive, but 'never' makes the sentence negative.

❌
❌ The Wrong Way

"I haven't never seen a ghost."

Don't use double negatives!
