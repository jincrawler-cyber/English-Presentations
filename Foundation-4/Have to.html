Grammar (+) and (-)

✅ Affirmative

I/You/We/They have to + verb
He/She/It has to + verb

❌ Negative

I/You/We/They don't have to + verb
He/She/It doesn't have to + verb

Questions (?)

We use the auxiliaries Do and Does to make questions, just like in the Present Simple!

Do you / they
have to work?
Does he / she
have to work?
*(Not "has to" in questions!)*
✅ Yes, I do. / No, I don't. ✅ Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
