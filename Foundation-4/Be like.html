The Secret The "Like" Trap

In English, the word "LIKE" has two completely different meanings!

❤️

1. "Like" = Verb

You use it for preferences and opinions.

"Do you like pizza?"
🎨

2. "Be Like" = Description

You ask this when you want someone to describe a thing or a person.

What + BE + noun + like?

Time Travel ⏱️

Because we use the verb TO BE, we can ask for descriptions in any tense!

Tense Question Answer (Description)
Present What 's the weather like? It's cold and windy. 🥶
Past What was the party like? It was great! 🎉
Future What will the course be like? It'll be hard work! 📚

DANGER: Common Mistake!

When answering these questions, never use the word "like" in your answer. Just use the verb TO BE + adjectives!

Don't say:

Q: What's the weather like?

A: It is like cold.

Say this:

Q: What's the weather like?

A: It is cold.

🗣️ Interview Time

Work in pairs. Ask your partner for descriptions. Remember the correct tense!

Student A asks:

  • 1. What is your favorite restaurant like?
  • 2. What was your last vacation like?
  • 3. What will your dream house be like?

Student B answers (Use adjectives!):

> It's very beautiful and... > It was hot, but fun! > It will be enormous...
