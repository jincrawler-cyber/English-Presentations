Connecting Ideas

Relative Pronouns: Who, Which, That

Read these sentences.
How do we connect them?

🤖 Robotic (2 sentences)

I know a man. The man makes the best chascas.

Look at the dog. The dog is running fast.

🗣️ Natural (1 sentence)

I know a man who makes the best chascas.

Look at the dog which is running fast.

The Rule The Magic Glue

Relative pronouns are like glue. They connect a noun to new information.

🧍‍♂️🧍‍♀️

WHO

For People

"I know the people who live there."
🐶🍔📱

WHICH

For Things & Animals

"I like the plants which grow there."

The Joker Card 🃏

Can't remember if it's who or which? There is a magic word that works for BOTH!

🃏

THAT

🧍

I know the people that live there.

🪴

I like the plants that grow there.

Position In the Middle!

The "glue" doesn't always go at the end. Sometimes it goes in the middle to describe the subject!

Example 1: Person

My brother, who is a doctor, lives in Mazatlan. 🏖️

Example 2: Thing

The car which is red is very fast. 🏎️
🥪

Tip: Think of it like a sandwich. The main sentence is the bread, and the relative pronoun is the delicious filling inside!
