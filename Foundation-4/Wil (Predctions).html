Grammar (+) and (-)

We use will to talk about what we think or guess will happen in the future.

✅ Affirmative

Subject + will + base verb
  • • I will pass the exam.
  • • She will be a doctor.
  • Contraction: I'll, She'll, We'll

❌ Negative

Subject + won't + base verb
  • • It won't rain tomorrow.
  • • They won't come to the party.
  • (won't = will not)

Questions (?)

To make questions, just move Will to the front!

Will + subject + base verb + ?
"Will robots take our jobs?"
Yes, they will. / I will.
No, they won't. / I won't.

Probability How sure are you?

We don't always know the future 100%. We can use these words to show probability:

100%

Will / Won't

"I will go to the party."

80%

Probably / Possibly

"I will probably go." / "I probably won't go."

*Note: 'Probably' goes after 'will', but before 'won't'!

50%

May / Might / Maybe

"I might go." / "Maybe I will go."

*May and Might do not use 'will'.

⭐ Bonus: Other uses of Will!

Will is not ONLY for predictions. Native speakers use it a lot for these three things:

⚡

1. Instant Decisions (Decisiones al momento)

"I'm thirsty. I will buy a soda." (No lo planeaste antes)

🤝

2. Promises (Promesas)

"Don't worry, I won't tell your secret." / "I will always love you."

🙋

3. Offers (Ofrecimientos de ayuda)

"Those bags look heavy. I will help you."
