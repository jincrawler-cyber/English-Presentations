What was happening? What interrupted it?

...when the phone rang. (Short action 🔴)
She was driving fast... (Long action 🟢)
...when she saw an accident. (Short action 🔴)

Observation: There are two types of actions!

🟢 The Past Continuous (The Background)

Usage: For long actions, or actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past.

Example: At 8:00 PM yesterday, I was eating dinner.

Grammar: WAS / WERE + Verb + ING

  • I / He / She / It + was sleeping
  • You / We / They + were working

🔴 The Past Simple (The Interruption)

Usage: For short actions, or actions that are complete.

Example: The phone rang.

Grammar: Verb in Past Form

  • Regular: Talked, Played, Started (+ed)
  • Irregular: Went, Came, Saw (Change)

The Connector: WHEN

It connects a long action to a short, interrupting action.

WHEN is always followed by the Past Simple (the short action).

I was studying WHEN the fire alarm went off.

She woke up WHEN the dog was barking.

Simple + WHEN + Continuous is possible, but less common!

The Connector: WHILE

It connects two long actions happening at the same time (parallel actions).

WHILE is always followed by the Past Continuous (the long action).

WHILE I was cooking, my brother was sleeping.

They were driving WHILE it was raining.

Key Rule Summary

Past Simple 🔴

Short action. Interrupts. Completed.

Used after: WHEN

Past Continuous 🟢

Long action. Background. In progress.

Used after: WHILE
