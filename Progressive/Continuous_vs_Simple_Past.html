Telling Stories

How to mix actions in the past

Tap here or click Next to start 🚀

Look at the tools we use to tell stories:

🎥

Video Camera

Records a long action happening over time.

Past Continuous
"We were walking..."
📸

Flash Camera

Captures a quick, interrupting event in a second.

Past Simple
"...the tire exploded."
🧠

The Golden Rule

Before we look at connectors, remember this:
Look at the action!

Duration = 🎥 (Continuous)
Interruption = 📸 (Simple)
Building the Video Camera 🎥

Read these messages carefully:

I was studying all night. 📚
Liar! You weren't studying. 📱
Were you watching TikTok again? 🤨

The Video Camera always needs TWO pieces:

WAS / WERE + Action-ING
🎥

The Past Continuous Matrix

1. Affirmative (+)

She was driving fast.

2. Negative (-)

She wasn't driving fast.

Just add NOT to the 'was/were'. The -ing stays!

3. Question (?)

Was she driving fast?

Flip the 'was/were' to the front!

WHILE = The Cameraman 🎥

WHILE means "during the time that". It demands duration. It must be followed by the video camera!

✅ While we were driving, it started to rain.
❌ While the car broke down, we were driving.
(A car doesn't break down slowly for hours!)

WHEN = The Director 🎬

Forget the old rules! WHEN is flexible. It simply sets the scene. Look at the verb right next to it:

Scene 1: Focusing on the interruption 📸

We were walking downtown when we got lost.

Scene 2: Setting the background action 🎥

When we were walking downtown, we got lost.

✨ Yes, this is 100% correct! "When" is opening the video camera scene.
