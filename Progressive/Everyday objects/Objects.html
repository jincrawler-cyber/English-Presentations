In My Bag

Vocabulary: Personal Items and Everyday Things

Uncountable

Some gum

Small pieces of food that you chew but do not swallow.

"I have some gum in my pocket to keep my breath fresh."
Countable

A candy bar

A sweet snack usually made of chocolate, nuts, or caramel.

"I am hungry. I want to buy a candy bar."
Countable

A water bottle

A container used to hold water so you can drink later.

"Always carry a water bottle when you travel."
Uncountable

Some hand lotion

A thick liquid (cream) that you put on your skin to make it soft.

"My hands are dry. I need some hand lotion."
Countable

An umbrella

A folding object that protects you from the rain or sun.

"It is raining outside! Don't forget your umbrella."
Uncountable / Plural

Some tissues

Soft paper used for cleaning your nose or hands.

"Do you have some tissues? I have a cold."
Countable

A hairbrush

An object with a handle used to make your hair neat.

"She uses a hairbrush every morning."
Countable

A mirror

A surface made of glass that allows you to see yourself.

"He looked in the mirror to check his hair."
Countable

A driver's license

An official card that says you are allowed to drive a car.

"You must show your driver's license to rent a car."
Countable

A receipt

A piece of paper that shows you paid for something in a shop.

"Keep your receipt if you want to return the product."
Uncountable

Some cash

Money in the form of coins or paper notes.

"I don't have a credit card. Can I pay with some cash?"
Countable

A keychain

A small metal ring used to hold your keys together.

"There is a small teddy bear on my keychain."
Countable

A passport

An official document needed to travel to other countries.

"You need a passport to fly from Spain to Mexico."
Plural

Sunglasses

Dark glasses that protect your eyes from the sun.

"It is very sunny at the beach. Wear your sunglasses!"
Plural

Headphones

An object you wear over your ears to listen to music privately.

"I am listening to music with my new headphones."
