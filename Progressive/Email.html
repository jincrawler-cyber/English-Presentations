How to Write an Introductory Email

Master paragraph structure and tone to present yourself successfully!

B1 English Level

1. What is a Paragraph?

The Golden Rule

A paragraph is a group of sentences that develop ONE single main idea. Organizing your email into paragraphs makes it clear and professional for the reader.

One Topic per Block

Keep all sentences in a paragraph focused on the same specific subject.

Start a New Paragraph

When you change the topic or want to talk about a new idea, press 'Enter'.

2. The Perfect Email Structure

Follow this logical order when introducing yourself to a new person:

1

Opening Sentences

Start with a friendly greeting and state the reason why you are writing the email.

Example: "I am writing to introduce myself..."
2

Information About Yourself

Explain who you are, what you do (your job, study, or project), and relevant details.

Example: "I work as an coordinator at..."
3

Questions & Showing Interest

Ask questions about the recipient or their country to build a friendly connection.

Example: "How is the weather over there?"
4

Closing Sentences

Say goodbye, mention future contact, and sign with your name.

Example: "Best regards, [Your Name]"

3. Dynamic Register Check

Choose the right tone depending on who you are writing to. Click the tabs below to explore the vocabulary banks:

Use when: Writing to relatives, close classmates, or people you already know well.

Hi there [Name],

How’s it going?

What have you been up to?

Let’s stay in touch.

Drop me a line soon!

Cheers,

Best wishes / Take care,

Use when: Writing to managers, clients, professors, or an international contact for the first time.

Dear Mr./Ms. [Surname],

It was a pleasure to hear from you.

I hope this email finds you well.

I am writing to introduce myself...

I look forward to hearing from you.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,

Yours sincerely / Yours faithfully,

4. Quick Challenge

Your Turn to Write!

Imagine you need to write an email introducing yourself to a distant relative who lives in another country. Write a short draft following these criteria:

  • Use an Informal Register.
  • Organize your email into exactly 3 distinct paragraphs.
  • Paragraph 1: Start with an informal greeting and say why you are writing.
  • Paragraph 2: Share 2 things about your current activities or routine.
  • Paragraph 3: Ask them about their country's weather and close the email nicely.
