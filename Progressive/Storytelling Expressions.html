The "Ghost" in the Computer Lab 👻💻
When I was in my second semester, I hated doing homework at home. It was finals week, and my friends and I were at the UNID campus working on a huge presentation.

One evening, almost everyone left and we stayed late in the lab. An hour later, the main lights in the hallway turned off. It was completely silent and very dark. I was telling my friends about the legend of the campus ghost. We were all a little nervous.

Then suddenly — out of the shadows at the door — a tall, dark figure appeared! We screamed and dropped our energy drinks! While we were panicking, the figure turned on a flashlight. It was just the night guard, telling us they were locking the doors! We felt so stupid, but at least we went for tacos after that.
