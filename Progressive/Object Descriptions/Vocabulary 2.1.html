Brand New

Used

VS

In Good Condition

Not damaged or showing signs of heavy wear.

Damaged

Harmed or impaired in a way that reduces value or functionality.

VS

Plain

Without patterns or decoration; simple.

Fancy

Elaborate in structure or decoration; luxurious.

VS

Outdated

No longer modern or current; old-fashioned.

Modern

Relating to the present or recent times as opposed to the remote past.

VS

Special

Better, greater, or otherwise different from what is usual.

Common

Occurring, found, or done often; prevalent.

VS

Useless

Having no use; not able to do anything useful.
