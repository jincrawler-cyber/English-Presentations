-ED vs. -ING Adjectives 🎭

"Who hurt you" vs. "Who is hurting you"

A survival guide to Feelings and Causes

Read the room...

Situation 1
POV: You are in a 3-hour math lecture. The professor speaks in a monotone voice.

"This class is so boring." 🥱 (The Villain)
Situation 2
POV: You are sitting in that class. You are falling asleep.

"I am so bored." 💀 (The Victim)

What is the difference? 🤔

Look closely at the two situations.

I am BORED (-ED)

  • Is it about how a person feels inside? Yes.
  • Is it an internal emotion? Yes.
  • Vibe: My current status / The victim

The class is BORING (-ING)

  • Does the class have feelings? No.
  • Does the class cause the feeling in you? Yes.
  • Vibe: The instigator / The Red Flag 🚩

The Anatomy (Where do they come from?) 🧬

These adjectives are actually born from VERBS (actions).

Base Word (VERB) Adjective -ED (How I feel) Adjective -ING (The Cause)
To surprise surprised 😲 surprising 🎁
To annoy annoyed 😒 annoying 🪰
To disappoint disappointed 😞 disappointing 📉
To embarrass embarrassed 😳 embarrassing 🤦‍♂️
To fascinate fascinated 🤩 fascinating 🌌
To frighten frightened 😨 frightening 👻
To shock shocked 🤯 shocking ⚡
To amuse amused 😏 amusing 🤡

The Formula 🧪

-ED ADJECTIVES
Describe emotions and feelings.
(Only humans and animals can feel these. A book cannot be bored!).
-ING ADJECTIVES
Describe the thing, situation, or person that CAUSES the emotion. (The characteristic of a noun).

Vibe Check: Choose the correct one ✅

1. POV: Your roommate's alarm has been ringing for 10 minutes non-stop.
➔ "This noise is so ________________ !" (annoy)

2. POV: You confidently answer a question in class, but you are 100% wrong.
➔ "My face was red, I was so ________________ ." (embarrass)

3. POV: The new movie had terrible CGI and a bad story.
➔ "Our trip to the cinema was really ________________ ." (disappoint)

The Receipts (Answers) 🧾

1. "This noise is so annoying!"
(The noise is the cause of the emotion).

2. "My face was red, I was so embarrassed."
(This is your internal feeling/emotion).

3. "Our trip to the cinema was really disappointing."
(The movie causes the disappointment in you).
