Predictions 🔮

"Trust me bro" vs. "I have receipts"

A survival guide to Will and Going to

Read the room...

Situation 1
POV: You and your friends are talking about the year 2050. Nobody knows anything for sure. You are just guessing.

"I think cars will fly." 🚗💨
Situation 2
POV: You look at the sky. It's completely dark gray. You hear thunder.

"It is going to rain." ⛈️

What is the difference? 🤔

Look closely at the two situations.

Cars WILL fly

  • Do we have proof today? No.
  • Is it a personal opinion? Yes.
  • Vibe: Delulu / Just a feeling

It IS GOING TO rain

  • Do we have proof today? Yes (dark clouds).
  • Is it based on current evidence? Yes.
  • Vibe: I have receipts 🧾

The Formula 🧪

WILL
Use it when you guess, think, or believe something will happen. No hard evidence.
(Usually comes with: I think, I hope, maybe, probably).
BE GOING TO
Use it when you can see, hear, or feel the evidence right now. A logical consequence.

The Anatomy (Grammar Check) 🦴

Let's break down the structure so you don't fail finals.

WILL (Base Verb)

Type Structure & Example
(+) Subj + will + Verb
I will pass the exam.
(-) Subj + won't + Verb
I won't fail this class.
(?) Will + Subj + Verb?
Will I survive this semester?

BE GOING TO (Base Verb)

Type Structure & Example
(+) Subj + am/is/are going to + Verb
She is going to study all night.
(-) Subj + am/is/are NOT going to + Verb
He isn't going to sleep.
(?) Am/Is/Are + Subj + going to + Verb?
Are we going to graduate?

Vibe Check: Choose the correct one ✅

1. POV: Your friend is carrying 10 coffees and trips over a cable.
➔ "Oh no! He ________________ drop them!"

2. POV: You are talking about artificial intelligence taking over the world.
➔ "In my opinion, robots ________________ take our jobs."

3. POV: You look at your phone. 1% battery.
➔ "My phone ________________ die."

The Receipts (Answers) 🧾

1. "Oh no! He is going to drop them!"
(Evidence: He is tripping right now).

2. "In my opinion, robots will take our jobs."
(Evidence: None, just a personal belief / "Trust me bro").

3. "My phone is going to die."
(Evidence: You literally see the 1% battery).
