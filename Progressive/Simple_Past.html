The Simple Past

The Ultimate Survival Guide.

Start the class 🚀

Part 1: The Affirmative (+)

Read what happened after class:

🤖
  • I crashed at my friend's house.
  • We studied all night.
👾
  • I woke up late. (wake)
  • I forgot my wallet. (forget)
Robots (Regulars) always end in -ED.
Rebels (Irregulars) change their spelling completely!
Part 2: The Plot Twist 🛑

Read these text messages:

I texted my ex last night. 😬
Good thing I didn't text mine. 😌
We went to the party. 🪩
I was tired. I didn't go. 😴

The past tense disappeared from the main verb!

"Texted" became text. "Went" became go.

Why? Because DIDN'T absorbed all the past energy. 🧛‍♂️

Part 3: Spilling the tea ☕

How do we ask for the gossip?

Did you finish the assignment? 📚

Did he buy the concert tickets? 🎟️

"DID" opens the door! 🚪

When "Did" is in the sentence, the action verb relaxes and stays in its normal form.

🕶️

The Golden Rule Matrix

Only ONE word can carry the past tense.

1. Affirmative (+)

She bought a coffee. (Irregular verb works here)

2. Negative (-)

She didn't buy a coffee.

"Didn't" is the boss. "Buy" goes back to normal.

3. Question (?)

Did she buy a coffee?

"Did" is the boss. "Buy" goes back to normal.

Final Exam! 🎮

Choose the correct grammar.

1. Yesterday I ____ (see) my teacher at the mall.

2. The movie was boring, so we ____ it.

3. Hey, ____ my Netflix password?

Nailed it!

To summarize:

✅ Affirmative (+) = Use your Robots (-ed) and Rebels.

🛑 Negative (-) & (?) = DID does all the work. The verb stays normal!
