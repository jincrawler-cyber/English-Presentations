Future Plans 🗓️

The "Oops" vs. The "It's in the Calendar"

Mastering Will and Present Continuous

Read the room... (Text Messages)

Situation 1
Oh no! We are completely out of milk for breakfast. 😭
Really? I will go to the OXXO right now to buy some! 🏃‍♂️
Situation 2
Hey! Are you free this Saturday to play video games? 🎮
Sorry bro, I am traveling to San Luis with Alma. We booked the hotel yesterday! 🚗

What is the difference? 🤔

When did they make the decision?

I WILL go...

  • Decided at the exact moment of speaking? Yes.
  • Is there a previous plan? No.
  • Vibe: Spontaneous / "I got this" 🦸‍♂️

I AM TRAVELING...

  • Decided at the exact moment of speaking? No.
  • Is there a previous plan/tickets? Yes.
  • Vibe: 100% Locked In 🔒

The Anatomy (Grammar Check) 🧬

WILL
(+) Subj + will + Base Verb
I will help you with that.
(-) Subj + won't + Base Verb
I won't tell anyone.
PRESENT CONTINUOUS
(+) Subj + am/is/are + Verb(ing)
She is meeting her boss at 3 PM.
(-) Subj + am/is/are NOT + Verb(ing)
We aren't working tomorrow.

The Formula 🧪

WILL
Use it for spontaneous decisions made exactly at the time of speaking. It's an instant reaction, an offer, or a promise.
(Usually reactions to a problem or a situation).
PRESENT CONTINUOUS
Use it for fixed arrangements in the near future. You already made the decision in the past, and usually, time/place are confirmed. (Keywords: tomorrow, at 8 PM, next weekend).

Vibe Check: Choose the correct one ✅

1. POV: The phone is ringing and everyone is busy.
➔ "Don't worry, I ________________ (answer) it!"

2. POV: You have an appointment card from your dentist for tomorrow at 4:00 PM.
➔ "I can't go to the cinema, I ________________ (see) the dentist tomorrow."

3. POV: You are at a restaurant. The waiter asks: "Are you ready to order?"
➔ "Yes, I ________________ (have) the hamburger and fries."

The Receipts (Answers) 🧾

1. "Don't worry, I will answer it!"
(Spontaneous reaction to the phone ringing).

2. "I can't go to the cinema, I am seeing the dentist tomorrow."
(Fixed arrangement/appointment already in your calendar).

3. "Yes, I will have the hamburger and fries."
(Spontaneous decision made at the moment you order).
