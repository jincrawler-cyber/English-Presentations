Milestones Vocabulary

Phase 1: The Beginning

Age 0 - 18

"In 2005, Alex came into the world in a city hospital. Later, he grew up in a small, quiet town near the sea."

1. To come into the world:

To be born

2. To grow up (be cared for):

To be raised

Phase 2: Learning

Age 6 - 22

"At age 6, Alex started his formal education. Many years later, he passed his final exams at the university and received his official diploma."

1. To attend classes:

To go to school

2. To finish university successfully:

To graduate

Phase 3: Independence

Age 23 - 28

"After university, Alex found a position at a tech company to earn money. With his first salary, he decided to rent his own apartment, without roommates."

1. To find employment:

To get a job

2. To live in a house on your own:

To live alone

Phase 4: Building a Life

Age 28 - 35

"Alex met Sam. They became very attracted to each other. He proposed, they had a big wedding, and years later, they started a family."

Attracted to someone

To be into someone

Promise to marry

To get engaged

Become spouses

To get married

Become parents

To have children

Phase 5: Golden Years

Age 65+

"Upon turning 65, Alex stopped working permanently. Soon after, his daughter had a baby, filling the house with a new generation."

1. To leave job due to old age:

To retire

2. When your child has a child:

To become a grandparent

Phase 6: Final Chapter

Age 80+

"After a beautiful journey full of memories, Alex reached the end of his life. He was surrounded by family when he peacefully left this world."

1. To die (respectful phrase):

To pass away
