Meeting Mutual Contacts

An interactive cheat-sheet for high- beginner to intermediate English learners.

1. Breaking the Ice

Phrases to use when you first greet someone you've heard about:

  • "I've heard a lot about you."
  • "I've heard good things about you."
  • "Jodi has told me so much about you."
  • "It's great to finally meet you in person."

2. How to Respond

Polite and modest replies when someone says they know you:

  • "Good things, I hope!"
  • "Oh, that's nice."
  • "Likewise! It's nice to meet you too."
  • "All good things, I'm sure!"

3. Making the Connection

Keep the talk going by focusing on your shared connection:

  • "So, how do you know Jodi?"
  • "How long have you known Jodi?"
  • "Where did you and Jodi meet?"

Conversation Flow

Mia: "Hi, I'm Mia."
You: "It's great to meet you, Mia. I've heard good things about you."
Mia: "Oh, that's nice. So, how do you know Jodi?"

Classroom Activity

Instructions: Find a classmate. Imagine you are both attending a social gathering or party organized by your mutual friend "David".

Your Goals:

  • Introduce yourself confidently
  • Use a natural "Icebreaker" phrase
  • Reply with a modest, friendly expression
  • Ask how they are connected to David
