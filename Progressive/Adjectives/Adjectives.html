Personality Adjectives

Vocabulary for how to describe people

Brave

A person who is not afraid of dangerous or difficult things.

"The warrior is very brave."

Cheerful

A person who is always happy, smiles a lot, and is positive.

"He is a cheerful boy; he always smiles."

Easygoing

A relaxed person who doesn't get angry or worried easily.

"My dad is easygoing. He never shouts."

Generous

A person who likes to give money, help, or gifts to others.

"She is very generous; she shares her lunch."

Helpful

A person who likes helping other people with their problems.

"The staff at the hotel are very helpful."

Honest

A person who always tells the truth and does not lie.

"Be honest. Did you give the wallet back?"

Intelligent

A person who is clever and learns new things quickly.

"Elephants are very intelligent animals."

Nervous

A person who feels worried or afraid about something.

"I always feel nervous before an English exam."

Reliable

A person you can trust to do something well or arrive on time.

"My car is old, but it is very reliable."

Selfish

A person who only thinks about themselves, not others.

"Don't be selfish! Share your toys with your brother."

Serious

A person who is quiet, thinks a lot, and doesn't joke much.

"Our history teacher is very serious in class."

Sociable

A person who likes being with others and meeting people.

"She's very sociable; she has many friends at school."

Creative

A person who is good at making art or has new ideas.

"Artists and writers are usually very creative."

Patient

A person who can wait for a long time without getting angry.

"Teachers need to be very patient with their students."

Shy

A person who is nervous or quiet when meeting new people.

"He was too shy to say hello to the new student."
