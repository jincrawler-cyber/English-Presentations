Part 1: Read and Observe 👀

Read these short conversations about shopping:

A: "I need a new bicycle. My old one is broken." 🚲

B: "These black shoes are okay, but I want to buy the brown ones." 👞

🤔 Think about it:

  • In conversation A, what word does one replace?
  • In conversation B, what word does ones replace?
  • Why do we use these words instead of repeating the noun?

Part 2: The Grammar Rule 📝

Using one and ones

Let's check your ideas!

  • In conversation A, one = bicycle (singular).
  • In conversation B, ones = shoes (plural).

The Rule: We use one (singular) and ones (plural) to avoid repeating a subject we have already mentioned. It makes our English sound more natural!

Part 3: Writing an Online Ad 🛒

Now, let's use what we learned to write a classified ad. Look at this example of someone looking for a specific item:

WANTED: VINTAGE CAMERA, 2 LENSES

I'm interested in buying an old film camera. Do you have one for sale? It doesn't have to be perfect, but I need one that works.

I'm also looking for two lenses. I want real glass ones, not cheap plastic ones.

I want these things because I am starting a photography course next month. I'll pay a good price!

Email me at: photo.student@email.com

Part 4: Your Turn to Write! ✍️

Steps for Writing a Great Ad:

  1. Write a short title: Leave out unimportant words like "I am looking for a...".
    (Example: "WANTED: VINTAGE CAMERA").
  2. Describe the item: Mention the condition, age, size, or color.
  3. Don't repeat words! Use one and ones to talk about the items.
  4. Give a reason: Explain why you want it (e.g., "I am starting a course"). It helps people connect with you.
  5. Include contact info: Tell people how to send you messages.

Final Challenge:

Write your own ad for something that is hard to find (a special video game, a rare book, specific clothes). Follow the 5 steps!
